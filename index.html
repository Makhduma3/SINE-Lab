About the lab

Research at the meeting point of AI and cybersecurity

Modern infrastructure, including industrial systems, smart environments, and critical services, increasingly relies on interconnected devices and edge computing. While these technologies improve connectivity and automation, they also introduce new cybersecurity challenges.

At SINE Lab (Security, Intelligence, Networks, and Edge), we develop AI-driven approaches to detect, analyze, and mitigate cyber threats in networked, IoT, and edge computing environments. Our research focuses on intelligent intrusion detection, federated learning, large language models (LLMs) for security analysis and decision support, and intent-aware systems that enable adaptive cyber defence.

We also investigate explainable and trustworthy AI to make security decisions more transparent, reliable, and understandable. Our goal is to move beyond traditional threat detection toward intelligent systems that can interpret security events and support effective responses.

Our research is supported by a physical IoT testbed developed and maintained by our research team, alongside experiments using publicly available cybersecurity datasets. This allows us to study real-world device behaviour, evaluate emerging cyber threats, and develop practical security solutions for connected and mission-critical environments.

Why "SINE"?

A sine wave represents a steady, predictable rhythm. Connected devices have rhythms too: sensors report data periodically, and controllers respond to commands. At SINE, we use artificial intelligence to understand these patterns, detect unusual activities, and develop intelligent, adaptive security solutions for networked and edge systems.

Research

Research Directions

  1. 01

    Intent-Aware IoT Testbeds and Cybersecurity Datasets

    We develop real-world IoT and edge computing testbeds to study device behaviour, network communication, and emerging cyber threats. Our research focuses on collecting network traffic alongside device activities and communication intent to create realistic, richly labelled datasets for developing and evaluating cybersecurity solutions.

    • IoT Testbeds
    • Intent-Aware Datasets
    • MQTT / CoAP
    • Network Traffic
    • Zeek
    Active
  2. 02

    AI-Driven Intrusion Detection and Threat Analysis

    We develop AI and machine learning approaches to detect and analyze cyberattacks in IoT networks and critical infrastructure. Our research investigates device behaviour profiling, anomaly detection, lightweight learning models, and cross-dataset generalization to improve threat detection across diverse network environments.

    • Intrusion Detection
    • Anomaly Detection
    • Device Profiling
    • Deep Learning
    • Edge AI
    Active
  3. 03

    Federated and Privacy-Preserving AI for Cybersecurity

    We investigate federated learning approaches that enable distributed devices and networks to collaboratively train AI models without directly sharing their raw data. Our research focuses on improving detection performance, model personalization, communication efficiency, and adaptability across heterogeneous IoT and edge environments.

    • Federated Learning
    • Distributed AI
    • Privacy
    • Personalization
    • Edge Computing
    Active
  4. 04

    Large Language Models for Cybersecurity

    We explore the use of large language models (LLMs) to support cyber threat analysis, security incident investigation, and intelligent decision-making. Our research investigates how LLMs can interpret security events, use knowledge graphs and structured evidence, generate meaningful explanations, and assist security analysts in understanding complex attacks.

    • LLMs
    • Knowledge Graphs
    • Security Reasoning
    • Incident Analysis
    • Agentic AI
    Active
  5. 05

    Explainable and Trustworthy AI for Security

    We investigate methods to make AI-based cybersecurity systems more transparent, reliable, and understandable. Our research focuses on explaining model predictions, evaluating the quality of AI-generated security explanations, and assessing whether security decisions are supported by relevant evidence.

    • Explainable AI
    • Trustworthy AI
    • SHAP
    • Evidence Grounding
    • AI Reliability
    Active
  6. 06

    Intent-Aware Adaptive Cyber Defence

    We develop intelligent security frameworks that move beyond attack detection toward adaptive threat mitigation and decision support. Our research investigates how communication intent, network conditions, and available computing resources can guide security decisions in IoT and edge environments, particularly for mission-critical applications.

    • Adaptive Cyber Defence
    • Intent-Aware Security
    • Threat Mitigation
    • Resource Management
    • Critical Infrastructure
    Active
  7. 07

    Game-Theoretic and AI-Assisted Security Decisions

    We investigate how game theory and artificial intelligence can support security decisions in environments where attackers and defenders have competing objectives. Our research explores the reliability of AI-generated security recommendations and strategies for allocating limited verification resources to reduce security risks.

    • Game Theory
    • Adversarial Decisions
    • AI Verification
    • Security Optimization
    • Human-AI Decisions
    Emerging

Research Infrastructure

IoT and Edge Security Testbed

The testbed provides a controlled environment for collecting realistic network traffic, developing intent-aware datasets, and evaluating intelligent security solutions for connected and mission-critical systems.

Testbed Architecture

1

Connected Devices

Sensors, RFID readers, cameras, smart lighting, and actuators generate data and communicate using different protocols.

2

Data Aggregation

Raspberry Pi-based services collect device data and support communication using MQTT, CoAP, and other IoT protocols.

3

Edge Monitoring

Edge computing nodes monitor network activity, capture traffic, and support security analysis using tools such as Zeek and Wireshark.

4

Security Datasets

Device activities, communication patterns, and network observations are used to develop labelled datasets for AI-based cybersecurity research.

Testbed Components

Component Devices and Technologies Research Purpose
Environmental Monitoring Temperature, gas sensors, Raspberry Pi Pico Periodic sensing and device behaviour analysis
Access Control RFID readers and experimental credentials Event-driven communication and security monitoring
Smart Devices Smart lighting, actuators, cameras Command-response and event-based communication
Communication Services MQTT, CoAP, and experimental protocol services Multi-protocol traffic collection and analysis
Edge Infrastructure Raspberry Pi, Zeek, Wireshark, Mosquitto Network monitoring, data collection, and security evaluation

Research Capabilities

  • Intent-Aware Data Collection: Capturing device communication patterns and associated network activities.
  • Device Behaviour Profiling: Studying normal and abnormal communication across heterogeneous IoT devices.
  • Cyberattack Analysis: Supporting controlled experiments to evaluate intrusion detection and threat analysis methods.
  • Edge AI Evaluation: Investigating lightweight and intelligent security solutions for resource-constrained systems.
  • Dataset Development: Generating labelled data for machine learning and cybersecurity research.

Privacy and Responsible Research

  • Experiments are conducted within a controlled laboratory environment.
  • RFID experiments use dedicated experimental credentials.
  • Camera-based experiments focus on device behaviour and network activity, with privacy safeguards.
  • Data collection is designed to minimize unnecessary personal information.
  • Research activities follow applicable institutional policies and ethical requirements.
